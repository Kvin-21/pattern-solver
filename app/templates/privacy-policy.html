Ezoic Services
This website uses the services of Ezoic Inc. (“Ezoic”), including to manage third-party interest-based advertising. Ezoic may employ a variety of technologies on this website, including tools to store and access data on your device.

When you visit this website, Ezoic may use cookies to track your activity. Cookies are used to ensure you get the best experience on this website.

A cookie is a small text file sent to your device by a web server that enables the website to remember information about your browsing activity. First-party cookies are created by the site you are visiting, while third-party cookies are created by domains other than the one you are visiting directly.

The following information may be collected, used, and stored in a cookie when serving personalized ads:

  • IP address
  • Operating system type and version
  • Device type
  • Language preferences
  • Web browser type
  • Email (in a hashed or encrypted form)
Ezoic and its partners may use this data in combination with information that has been independently collected to deliver targeted advertisements across various platforms and websites. Ezoic’s partners typically include entities engaged in advertising, technology, and analytics.

You have the right to control the use of cookies on your device. You can set your browser to block all cookies, or to alert you when cookies are being sent. However, please note that blocking cookies may affect your experience on this website.
